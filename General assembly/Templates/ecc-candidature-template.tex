A written candidature has two parts: this Election and Control Committee (ECC) assessment, and the candidate's own statement (Templates/candidate-consent-template.tex). Use one form per candidate. This form applies once the ECC (New Bylaws \S13) is in place and administering the election; for elections run without an ECC, only the candidate's own written candidature is used.

This document records the Election and Control Committee's part of a written candidature; it does not itself elect the candidate, and does not substitute for the candidate's own consent and statement.
