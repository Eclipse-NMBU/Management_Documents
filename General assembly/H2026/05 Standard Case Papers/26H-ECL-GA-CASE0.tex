  \hline
  Background           & Old Bylaws \S4.8 requires the General Assembly to open by electing a Meeting Chair, Secretary, Vote Counters and Protocol Signers. This is one Case, not four. The number of eligible voters present is recorded as part of constituting the meeting. \\
  \hline
  Proposal             & The General Assembly resolves, in order: (a) to elect a Meeting Chair (ordstyrer): until elected, the person opening the meeting leads this election; (b) to elect a Secretary (referent); (c) to elect two Vote Counters (tellekorps), neutral in the Cases they count; (d) to elect two Protocol Signers (protokollunderskrivere) from among those present; (e) to record the number of eligible voters present. \\
  \hline
  External information & No further Case may be validly decided until the meeting is constituted. The Notice of Meeting and the Agenda are approved separately, under Cases 2 and 3. Attachments: Notice of Meeting (ECL-H2026-NOT-01), Agenda (ECL-H2026-AGD-01). \\
