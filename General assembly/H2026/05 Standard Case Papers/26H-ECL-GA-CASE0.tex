  \hline
  C-suite deliberation & The C-suite's deliberation on the Constitution of the Meeting concerns the proposals for the different meeting roles set out below, and their acceptance by the General Assembly. \\
  \hline
  Background           & Old Bylaws \S4.8 requires the General Assembly to open by electing a Meeting Chair, Secretary, Vote Counters and Protocol Signers, and by confirming that the Notice of Meeting and the Agenda were properly issued. These steps are treated as a single Case, not several, because they are all procedural conditions for validly opening the meeting. The number of eligible voters present is recorded as part of constituting the meeting. \\
  \hline
  Proposal             & The General Assembly resolves, in order:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.25em, topsep=0.2em]
    \item to elect a Meeting Chair (ordstyrer): until elected, the person opening the meeting leads this election;
    \item to elect a Secretary (referent);
    \item to elect two Vote Counters (tellekorps), neutral in the Cases they count;
    \item to elect two Protocol Signers (protokollunderskrivere) from among those present;
    \item to record the number of eligible voters present;
    \item to approve the Notice of Meeting, 26H-ECL-GA-NOT1, confirming it was distributed to members at least two weeks before this meeting (no later than 16 September 2026);
    \item to approve the Agenda, 26H-ECL-GA-AGD1, as the order of business for this meeting.
  \end{enumerate} \\
  \hline
  External information & No further Case may be validly decided until the meeting is constituted. If the Notice of Meeting was not distributed within the required two-week deadline, the General Assembly cannot validly proceed. Attachments: Notice of Meeting (26H-ECL-GA-NOT1), Agenda (26H-ECL-GA-AGD1). \\
  \hline
\end{tabularx}
