  \hline
  Background & Old Bylaws \S4.8 requires the General Assembly to open by electing a Meeting Chair, Secretary, Vote Counters and Protocol Signers. This is one Case, not four. Approval of the Notice and Agenda, and verification of eligible voters present, are handled here too, as matters necessary to constitute the meeting. \\
  \hline
  Proposal & The General Assembly resolves, in order: (a) to elect a Meeting Chair (ordstyrer) -- until elected, the person opening the meeting leads this election; (b) to elect a Secretary (referent); (c) to elect two Vote Counters (tellekorps), neutral in the Cases they count; (d) to elect two Protocol Signers (protokollunderskrivere) from among those present; (e) to approve the Notice of Meeting, ECL-H2026-NOT-01 (Case ECL-H2026-CASE-01); (f) to approve the Agenda, ECL-H2026-AGD-01 (Case ECL-H2026-CASE-02); (g) to record the number of eligible voters present. \\
  \hline
  External information & No further Case may be validly decided until the meeting is constituted. Attachments: Notice of Meeting (ECL-H2026-NOT-01), Agenda (ECL-H2026-AGD-01). \\
  \hline
\end{tabularx}
