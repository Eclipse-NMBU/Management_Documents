\noindent\textbf{Document ID:} ECL-H2026-MEMB-01\\
\textbf{Applies from:} the New Bylaws' entry into force (proposed New Bylaws \S17.2), i.e. once the Bylaw Amendment (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A) is adopted and this Protocol (ECL-H2026-PROT-01) is signed. \textbf{Governing Bylaws:} proposed New Bylaws \S5.2 (Admission and Membership Contract), \S5.4 (Mentors and Alumni), \S7 (Privacy).

\vsection{1. Parties}
Between \textbf{Eclipse NMBU} (the Association) and the undersigned member.

\vsection{2. What the member confirms by signing}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item That they have read the Bylaws (proposed New Bylaws \S5.2.1).
  \item That they understand and accept the risk associated with spaceflight activity (risk-acceptance clause, \S5.2.1).
  \item That they understand the membership tiers, rights and duties in \S3 below.
\end{enumerate}

\vsection{3. Membership structure}
The member is admitted as an \textbf{Active Member} once this contract is signed and, per \S5.2.2--3, an admission interview conducted by two Board members has been completed (the CPO is responsible for the process). Membership tiers, and the rights and duties attached to each, follow proposed New Bylaws \S5.1, \S5.5 and \S5.6, and are summarised in the Glossary (ECL-H2026-GLOSS-01, ``Membership tiers'').

The membership period runs from 1 August to 31 July, per \S5.3; a member may join at any time within the period, but membership ends 31 July unless renewed. Mentor and Alumni status are exempt from this fixed period, per \S5.3--\S5.4.

\vsection{4. Organisational information and records}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{What is stored:} the Association stores the personal data necessary and relevant to administering membership (proposed New Bylaws \S7.1) -- at minimum name, contact details, membership tier history, and role/office history.
  \item \textbf{What is publicly displayed:} by default, for members who become \textbf{Alumni}, the Association's website displays the person's \textbf{name, the role(s)/office(s) held, and the years/semesters active in each role}. No other personal data is displayed publicly without the person's separate consent. \emph{[NOTE -- this default is the Board-confirmed answer used throughout this document set (see the Clarifications Needed note, ECL-H2026-README); it is not yet written into the Bylaws text itself, since \S7 of the proposed New Bylaws covers deletion/opt-out rights but not default public-display fields. The Board should consider whether to add this as a Standing Order under \S17.4, or a future Bylaw Amendment.]}
  \item \textbf{Alumni records:} a member automatically becomes Alumni when their Membership Contract lapses without renewal (\S5.4.2). Alumni records are maintained on the website per point 2 above, alongside the organisation's historical record of who has held which office, in support of the organisational-sustainability principle in \S2.5.
  \item \textbf{Opt-out:} the member, or a former member/Alumnus, may at any time request that their personal data -- including anything displayed publicly under point 2 -- be deleted, per \S7.3. The request is made in writing to the CPO; deletion is carried out without undue delay, with written confirmation sent to the requester.
  \item \textbf{Legal/organisational limitations on the opt-out:} the Association may retain the minimum records legally required (e.g. for accounting purposes) and the minimum record needed to evidence past Bylaw compliance (e.g. that a given office was validly filled at a given General Assembly, per the Protocol, ECL-H2026-PROT-01) even after a deletion request; such retained records are not publicly displayed once deletion is requested.
\end{enumerate}
