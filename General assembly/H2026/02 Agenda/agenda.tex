\vsection{Standing order (Old Bylaws \S4.8)}
The Agenda follows the fixed order below. Items (e) onward are Standard Case Papers (ECL-H2026-CASE-*); items with a submitted Case number use that Case's own Document ID.

\begin{enumerate}[label=Case [TBD].]
  \item \textbf{Constitution of the Meeting (konstituering)} -- ECL-H2026-CASE-00. One Case, covering:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.]
    \item Election of Meeting Chair (ordstyrer)
    \item Election of Secretary (referent)
    \item Election of Vote Counters (tellekorps) -- two members
    \item Election of Protocol Signers (protokollunderskrivere)
    \item Approval of the Notice of Meeting (godkjenning av innkalling) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-01
    \item Approval of the Agenda (godkjenning av saksliste) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-02
    \item Verification of the number of eligible voters present
  \end{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Approval of previous semester's financial statements} (godkjenning av regnskap fra forrige semester) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-03.
  \item \textbf{Approval of coming semester's budget} (godkjenning av budsjett for kommende semester) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-04.
  \item \textbf{Semester Report / Chair's Report} (semesterberetning / styreleders beretning) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-05.
  \item \textbf{[Submitted Cases, in the order received]} -- each Case Paper carries its own Document ID (ECL-H2026-CASE-NN), assigned as Cases are received. Amendments to any Case are presented together with that Case per the Amendment Submission Procedure (ECL-H2026-PROC-01).
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment} (vedtektsendringsforslag) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-07A/07B/07C. Voted per the two-stage procedure in ECL-H2026-CASE-07C.
  \item \textbf{Transitional C-suite Case} -- ECL-H2026-CASE-08. Conditional on the outcome of the Bylaw Amendment; fixes which four roles are open in the following Election case.
  \item \textbf{Election of Board Members} (valg av styremedlemmer) -- ECL-H2026-CASE-06. Conducted per Old Bylaws \S4.7, using the ballots in ECL-H2026-BALLOT-02A (if the Bylaw Amendment passed) or ECL-H2026-BALLOT-02B (if it failed).
  \item \textbf{Other business} (eventuelt annet).
\end{enumerate}

\vsection{Note on ordering}
The Bylaw Amendment and Transitional C-suite Case are placed before the Election so that the electorate knows, when voting on candidates, which four roles are actually being filled. Submitted Cases unrelated to the Bylaws or elections are placed before the Bylaw Amendment so that routine business is not held hostage to the outcome of the constitutional vote.
