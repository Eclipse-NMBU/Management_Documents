\noindent\textbf{Document/Protocol ID:} 26H-ECL-GA-PROT2\\
\meetingline\\
\textbf{Status:} the authoritative record of what the General Assembly decided. Completed live by the Secretary during the meeting; finalised on signature by the two Protocol Signers. Proxy voting is prohibited (Old Bylaws \S4.3), so no proxy form (fullmakt) is used.\\
\textbf{Cross-references:} every Case Paper and Amendment referenced below carries its own Document ID; this Protocol is in turn the source Protocol ID cited by any Responsibility Contract arising from a General Assembly resolution (see 26H-ECL-GA-RESP1B, \S ``General Assembly $\to$ CEO'').\\
\textbf{Voting method:} only the Election of Board Members (Case 7) is voted on by ballot. Every other Case, Amendment and the Bylaw Amendment (Case 5) is voted on by show of hands, with each Eligible Voter's voter number recorded against their vote where a count is required.
