\vspace{0.2cm}
Use one form per candidate, for the office they are standing for. Required under Old Bylaws \S4.7 / New Bylaws \S11.2: ``A candidate must have consented to the candidature. Candidates who cannot attend may stand with a written candidature.'' Submit to the Board by the Advance Nomination deadline in the Notice of Meeting (ECL-H2026-NOT-01), or hand to the Meeting Chair before the relevant Election Case if nominated from the floor (benkeforslag).
