\noindent\textbf{Document ID:} ECL-H2026-CASE-07C\\
\meetingline\\
\textbf{Status:} attachment to Case 7 (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A). This procedure sets out how the vote required by Old Bylaws \S4.6 (``vedtektsendringer kan bare skje når 2/3 av medlemmene stemmer for endringen'') and the vote on any Amendments to the proposed text are taken at this General Assembly. It is part of the Voting Order (ECL-H2026-VOTORD-01), which the Meeting Chair proposes and the General Assembly approves before voting on Cases begins.

\vsection{Why two stages}
Old Bylaws \S4.6 sets a single threshold, 2/3 of all members, for the decision to change the Bylaws. The proposed New Bylaws (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A) is a large document; members may want to adopt the overall restructuring while still changing specific wording. Voting on every clause individually at 2/3 of all members would be impractical, and \S4.6 attaches the 2/3 threshold to ``the change'' as a whole, not to each clause. The procedure below therefore separates the constitutional question (do we adopt New Bylaws at all) from the drafting question (what exact wording), while keeping the number of separate votes as small as possible.

\vsection{Stage 1: Whether to adopt New Bylaws at all}
\textbf{Question put to the vote:} ``Shall Eclipse NMBU adopt the proposed New Bylaws (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A) to replace the Old Bylaws, subject to any Amendments the General Assembly adopts in Stage 2?''

\textbf{Threshold:} 2/3 of \emph{all} members of Eclipse NMBU (not merely those present or voting), per Old Bylaws \S4.6.

\textbf{If Stage 1 is not adopted:} Case 7 is closed and the Old Bylaws remain in force unchanged. Stage 2 is not reached.

\textbf{If Stage 1 is adopted:} the General Assembly proceeds to Stage 2. No separate final ratification vote is taken after Stage 2: the Stage 1 ``Yes'' is the adoption, at the required 2/3 threshold, of the New Bylaws as they stand once Stage 2's Amendments are resolved. The total number of votes is therefore Stage 1 plus one vote per submitted Amendment.

\vsection{Stage 2: Submitted Amendments to the proposed text}
Each Amendment submitted under the Amendment Submission Procedure (ECL-H2026-PROC-01) against the proposed New Bylaws text is presented and voted on individually, in the order set by the Voting Order (ECL-H2026-VOTORD-01).

\textbf{Threshold per Amendment: simple majority of votes cast.} The 2/3 threshold in Old Bylaws \S4.6 applies to the decision to change the Bylaws, which is taken in Stage 1.

If multiple Amendments target the same clause, they are voted on from most to least far-reaching, per the Voting Order.

\vsection{Result}
Once every submitted Amendment has been voted on, the text of ECL-H2026-CASE-07A, as amended, is the adopted New Bylaws. \textbf{The New Bylaws take effect when the Meeting Protocol (ECL-H2026-PROT-01) is signed}, unless the General Assembly resolves otherwise.
