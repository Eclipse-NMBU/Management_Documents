  \hline
  Proposal             & The General Assembly resolves that the roles to be elected under Case 9 (ECL-H2026-CASE-06B) are CEO, CTO, CFO and CMO, as defined in Old Bylaws \S5.1--\S5.5. The election is conducted under the election mechanics in Old Bylaws \S4.7: majority vote per role; a second round if no candidate achieves a majority; blank votes counted in the mass, with the option to remove blank votes from the mass in a further round per \S4.7.2. \\
