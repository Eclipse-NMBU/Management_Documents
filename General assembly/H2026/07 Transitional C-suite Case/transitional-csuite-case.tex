\vsection{Background}
The roles being elected at this General Assembly depend on whether the Bylaw Amendment (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A) passes. Rather than write two separate Election Cases, this Case fixes -- conditionally -- which four roles the following Election Case elects into, so the electorate knows exactly what they are voting for before the Election Case opens.

\vsection{Proposed Resolution}
The General Assembly resolves that the roles to be elected under the Election of Board Members Case, ECL-H2026-CASE-06, are:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{If the Bylaw Amendment (ECL-H2026-CASE-07A, as amended) passed in Stage 1 of ECL-H2026-CASE-07C:} CEO, CTO, CBO and CPO, as defined in the proposed New Bylaws \S12.1.
  \item \textbf{If the Bylaw Amendment failed:} CEO, CTO, CFO and CMO, as defined in Old Bylaws \S5.1--\S5.5 -- i.e. the current structure continues unchanged.
\end{itemize}

\vsection{Election mechanics}
In both branches, the election itself is conducted under \textbf{Old Bylaws \S4.7 election mechanics} (majority vote per role; a second round if no candidate achieves a majority; blank votes counted in the mass, with the option to remove blank votes from the mass in a further round per \S4.7.2), \textbf{not} the New Bylaws' Election and Control Committee process (\S11 of ECL-H2026-CASE-07A). This is because the Election and Control Committee does not yet exist -- it can only be created and elected at a future Supplementary General Assembly once the New Bylaws (if adopted) are in force. Even if the Bylaw Amendment passes at this meeting, \textbf{this meeting's own election} still runs on Old Bylaws \S4.7 mechanics.
