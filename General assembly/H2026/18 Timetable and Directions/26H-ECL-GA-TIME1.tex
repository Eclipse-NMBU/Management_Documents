\vsection{2. Venue}
The meeting is held in \meetinglocation. Festsalen is a function room on the NMBU campus in Ås, Norway.

\vsection{3. How to get there}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{By train:} regular trains run from Oslo S to Ås station on the Østfold Line (Østfoldbanen), roughly 25--30 minutes. From Ås station, the NMBU campus is a short walk (around 1.5 km, roughly 15--20 minutes) or a short local bus ride.
  \item \textbf{By bus:} local buses connect Ås station and Ås town centre to the NMBU campus; check current routes and times with Ruter (Oslo/Akershus/Viken area public transport) before travelling, since routes and timetables change between semesters.
  \item \textbf{By car:} limited visitor parking is available on campus; members driving should allow extra time to park and walk to Festsalen.
  \item \textbf{By bike:} Ås town centre and the NMBU campus are connected by cycle paths; racks are available across campus.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{On campus:} Festsalen is in the Main Building (Urbygningen), at the centre of campus. Signposting and campus maps are available at the main entrances; ask any staffed reception point on the way in if the room is not immediately obvious.

For last-minute questions (registration issues, finding the room, accessibility needs), contact the Board at post@eclipsenmbu.no or via the channel given in the Notice of Meeting, 26H-ECL-GA-NOT1.
