\noindent\textbf{Document ID:} 26H-ECL-GA-CASE4C\\
\meetingline\\
\textbf{Status:} attachment to Case 5 (26H-ECL-GA-CASE4A). This procedure sets out how the vote required by Old Bylaws \S4.6 (``vedtektsendringer kan bare skje når 2/3 av medlemmene stemmer for endringen'') and the vote on any Amendments to the proposed text are taken at this General Assembly. It is part of the Voting Order (26H-ECL-GA-VOTORD1), which the Meeting Chair proposes and the General Assembly approves before voting on Cases begins.

\vsection{Why two stages}
Old Bylaws \S4.6 sets a single threshold, 2/3 of all members, for changing the Bylaws. The proposed New Bylaws (26H-ECL-GA-CASE4A) is a large document, and members may want to adopt the overall restructuring while still changing specific wording. Voting on every clause individually at 2/3 of all members would be impractical, and \S4.6 attaches that threshold to ``the change'' as a whole, not to each clause. The procedure below therefore splits the vote in two: first whether to adopt New Bylaws at all, then what the exact wording should be, using as few separate votes as possible.

\vsection{Stage 1: Whether to adopt New Bylaws at all}
\textbf{Question put to the vote:} ``Shall Eclipse NMBU adopt the proposed New Bylaws (26H-ECL-GA-CASE4A) to replace the Old Bylaws, subject to any Amendments the General Assembly adopts in Stage 2?''

\textbf{Threshold:} 2/3 of \emph{all} members of Eclipse NMBU (not merely those present or voting), per Old Bylaws \S4.6.

\textbf{Voting method:} show of hands, with each Eligible Voter's voter number recorded against their vote; no separate ballot paper is used, since Old Bylaws \S4.6 does not require secret voting for this Case. The vote count and result are recorded directly in the Meeting Protocol (26H-ECL-GA-PROT1).

\textbf{If Stage 1 is not adopted:} Case 5 is closed and the Old Bylaws remain in force unchanged. Stage 2 is not reached.

\textbf{If Stage 1 is adopted:} the General Assembly proceeds to Stage 2. No separate final ratification vote is taken after Stage 2: the Stage 1 ``Yes'' is the adoption, at the required 2/3 threshold, of the New Bylaws as they stand once Stage 2's Amendments are resolved. The total number of votes is therefore Stage 1 plus one vote per submitted Amendment.

\vsection{Stage 2: Submitted Amendments to the proposed text}
Each Amendment submitted under the Amendment Submission Procedure (26H-ECL-GA-PROC1) against the proposed New Bylaws text is presented and voted on individually, in the order set by the Voting Order (26H-ECL-GA-VOTORD1).

\textbf{Threshold per Amendment: simple majority of votes cast.} The 2/3 threshold in Old Bylaws \S4.6 applies to the decision to change the Bylaws, which is taken in Stage 1. Amendments are Case proposals, not candidate elections, so they too are decided by show of hands with voter numbers recorded, not by ballot.

If multiple Amendments target the same clause, they are voted on from most to least far-reaching, per the Voting Order.

\vsection{Result}
Once every submitted Amendment has been voted on, the text of 26H-ECL-GA-CASE4A, as amended, is the adopted New Bylaws. \textbf{The New Bylaws take effect when the Meeting Protocol (26H-ECL-GA-PROT1) is signed}, unless the General Assembly resolves otherwise.
