Use one form per candidate, for whichever office or Election Case they are standing for: a Board role, a role on the Election and Control Committee, or any other office elected by the General Assembly. Required under Old Bylaws \S4.7 / New Bylaws \S11.2: ``A candidate must have consented to the candidature. Candidates who cannot attend may stand with a written candidature.'' Submit to the Board before the meeting if nominated in advance (forhåndsbenking), or hand to the Meeting Chair before the relevant Election Case opens if nominated from the floor (benkeforslag). This is a general-purpose form: it is not specific to any one office or Case.
