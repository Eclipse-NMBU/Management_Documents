\vspace{0.2cm}
One entry per signed Responsibility \& Signature Contract (26H-ECL-GA-RESP1A or -1B). Per proposed New Bylaws \S14.11, every document, including one signed on paper, must also be stored digitally; the Board Record's job is therefore to index where that digital copy is kept, not to re-state the contract's terms. Per \S14/\S5.1, the transfer is effective on signature by From and To and is considered approved by default -- this log does not itself approve the transfer, it records that it took place and where the digital copy of the signed contract can be found.
