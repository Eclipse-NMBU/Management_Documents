\vsection{1. Parties}
Between \textbf{Eclipse NMBU} (the Association) and the undersigned member.

\vsection{2. What the member confirms by signing}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item That they have read the Bylaws (proposed New Bylaws \S5.2.1).
  \item That they understand and accept the risk associated with spaceflight activity (risk-acceptance clause, \S5.2.1).
  \item That they understand the membership tiers, rights and duties in \S3 below.
  \item That they consent, on an opt-out basis, to the Association displaying and using their information and image as set out in \S4 point 6, unless and until they opt out under \S4 point 4.
\end{enumerate}

\vsection{3. Membership structure}
The member is admitted as an \textbf{Active Member} once this contract is signed and, per \S5.2.2--3, an admission interview conducted by two Board members has been completed (the CPO is responsible for the process). Membership tiers, and the rights and duties attached to each, follow proposed New Bylaws \S5.1, \S5.5 and \S5.6, and are summarised in the Glossary (26H-ECL-GA-GLOSS1, ``Membership tiers'').

The membership period runs from 1 August to 31 July, per \S5.3; a member may join at any time within the period, but membership ends 31 July unless renewed. Mentor and Alumni status are exempt from this fixed period, per \S5.3--\S5.4.

\vsection{4. Organisational information and records}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{What is stored:} the Association stores the personal data necessary and relevant to administering membership (proposed New Bylaws \S7.1): at minimum name, contact details, membership tier history, and role/office history.
  \item \textbf{What is publicly displayed:} by default, for members who become \textbf{Alumni}, the Association's website displays the person's \textbf{name, the role(s)/office(s) held, and the years/semesters active in each role}. No other personal data is displayed publicly without the person's separate consent, except as set out in point 6 below.
  \item \textbf{Alumni records:} a member automatically becomes Alumni when their Membership Contract lapses without renewal (\S5.4.2). Alumni records are maintained on the website per point 2 above, alongside the organisation's historical record of who has held which office, in support of the organisational-sustainability principle in \S2.5.
  \item \textbf{Opt-out:} the member, or a former member/Alumnus, may at any time request that their personal data: including anything displayed publicly under point 2 or point 6: be deleted, per \S7.3. The request is made in writing to the CPO; deletion is carried out without undue delay, with written confirmation sent to the requester.
  \item \textbf{Legal/organisational limitations on the opt-out:} the Association may retain the minimum records legally required (e.g. for accounting purposes) and the minimum record needed to evidence past Bylaw compliance (e.g. that a given office was validly filled at a given General Assembly, per the Protocol, 26H-ECL-GA-PROT1) even after a deletion request; such retained records are not publicly displayed once deletion is requested.
  \item \textbf{Public profile and media (all members):} unless the member opts out under point 4, the Association may display the member's CV and other information about the member's role(s) and activity in the Association on the Association's website and social media, and may use the member's picture and other information about the member that is public, or that the member has said publicly, across the Association's own and shared channels (e.g.\ sponsor materials, event coverage). This applies to all members from admission, not only to Alumni, and is broader than the Alumni default in point 2. Consent to this point is given by signing this contract (\S2), and remains in force unless and until the member opts out under point 4.
\end{enumerate}
