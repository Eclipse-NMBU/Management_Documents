\vsection{2. Nature of this General Assembly}
This is a General Assembly with elections (valggeneralforsamling): all four Board (styret) positions are up for election, per Old Bylaws \S4.7.

\textbf{A Bylaw Amendment (vedtektsendring) is on the agenda.} The General Assembly will be asked to consider replacing the current Bylaws (Old Bylaws) with the proposed New Bylaws (nye vedtekter), see the Bylaw Amendment Proposal, ECL-H2026-CASE-07A/07B. Adoption requires 2/3 of all members of Eclipse NMBU to vote in favour, per Old Bylaws \S4.6.

\textbf{The C-suite election is on the agenda.} Because the outcome of the Bylaw Amendment vote determines which C-suite structure is being elected into, a Transitional C-suite Case (ECL-H2026-CASE-08) precedes the Election of Board Members case and fixes which roles are open depending on whether the Bylaw Amendment passes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{If the Bylaw Amendment passes:} CEO, CTO, CBO, CPO are elected.
  \item \textbf{If the Bylaw Amendment fails:} CEO, CTO, CFO, CMO are elected (i.e. the current structure continues).
\end{itemize}
In both cases, election follows the Old Bylaws \S4.7 election mechanics (the Election and Control Committee introduced by the New Bylaws does not yet exist and cannot run this election).

\vsection{5. Voting rights}
Per Old Bylaws \S10.2, Board Members, Core Members and Active Members have voting rights and are eligible for office. All members, including Inactive Members, have the right to attend. Proxy voting is not permitted (Old Bylaws \S4.3; also prohibited under the New Bylaws \S8.4.3) -- no member may vote on another member's behalf, in person or by any form of power of attorney.
