\vsection{2. Nature of this General Assembly}
This is a General Assembly with elections (valggeneralforsamling): all four Board (styret) positions are up for election, per Old Bylaws \S4.7.

\textbf{A Bylaw Amendment (vedtektsendring) is on the agenda as Case 7.} The General Assembly will be asked to replace the current Bylaws (Old Bylaws) with the proposed New Bylaws (nye vedtekter); see the Bylaw Amendment Proposal, ECL-H2026-CASE-07A/07B/07C. Adoption requires 2/3 of all members of Eclipse NMBU to vote in favour, per Old Bylaws \S4.6.

\textbf{The election of the Board is on the agenda as Cases 8 and 9.} Case 8 fixes the C-suite structure that follows from the result of Case 7, and Case 9 elects the four Board members into that structure. The election follows the Old Bylaws \S4.7 election mechanics. See the Agenda, ECL-H2026-AGD-01.

\vsection{3. Deadlines}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Case Papers (sakspapirer)} are available to members no later than \textbf{23 September 2026}, one week before the General Assembly, per Old Bylaws \S4.5.
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment proposal:} must reach the Board no later than \textbf{23 September 2026}, one week before the General Assembly, per Old Bylaws \S4.6. It is published with the Case Papers.
  \item \textbf{Amendments (endringsforslag)} to a Case on the Agenda must be submitted in writing before voting on Cases begins at the meeting. See the Amendment Submission Procedure, ECL-H2026-PROC-01.
  \item \textbf{Advance Nominations (forhåndsbenking):} candidates may notify the Board before the meeting that they are standing. Candidates who cannot attend in person may submit a written candidature; see Candidate Documentation, ECL-H2026-CAND-01. Nomination from the floor (benkeforslag) remains possible during the meeting itself.
\end{itemize}

\vsection{4. Case Papers}
Case Papers, including the Agenda, the full Bylaw Amendment Proposal and all Standard Case Papers, are made available to members no later than 23 September 2026, per Old Bylaws \S4.5. The Overview, ECL-H2026-OVW-01, lists every document in the set.

\vsection{5. Voting rights}
Per Old Bylaws \S10.2, Board Members, Core Members and Active Members have voting rights and are eligible for office. All members, including Inactive Members, have the right to attend. Proxy voting is not permitted (Old Bylaws \S4.3): no member may vote on another member's behalf, in person or by any form of power of attorney.
