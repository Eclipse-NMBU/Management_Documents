   & \rule{0pt}{1.2cm}\textbf{Background.} The Bylaw Amendment was adopted under Case 5. New Bylaws \S 12.1 sets the Board as four members: CEO (Chief Executive Officer), CTO (Chief Technical Officer), CBO (Chief Business Officer) and CPO (Chief People Officer). This Case fixes the roles before the Election of Board Members (Case 7) opens, so members know which roles they are voting on.\par\medskip
  \textbf{Proposal.} The General Assembly resolves that the roles to be elected under Case 7 (26H-ECL-GA-CASE6A) are CEO, CTO, CBO and CPO, as defined in New Bylaws \S 12.1. The election is conducted under the election mechanics in Old Bylaws \S 4.7: majority vote per role; a second round if no candidate achieves a majority; blank votes counted in the mass, with the option to remove blank votes from the mass in a further round per \S 4.7.2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    \\
