\vspace{0.2cm}
Under Old Bylaws \S11.1, Signature Right (signaturrett) is held \textbf{jointly by the Board}: ``Det er styrets medlemmer som i fellesskap innehar signaturrett.'' Where the whole Board has jointly agreed to sign, it is sufficient for either the CEO (Styreleder) or the CFO (Økonomiansvarlig) to carry out the actual signing. The sitting Board remains responsible for all agreements in force (Old Bylaws \S11.1). There is no individual delegation mechanism under the Old Bylaws -- this form records the Board's joint decision to sign, not a transfer of authority to one person.
