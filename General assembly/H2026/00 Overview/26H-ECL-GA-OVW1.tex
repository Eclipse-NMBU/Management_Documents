\vsection{4. After the Bylaw Amendment vote}
Case 5 decides which documents are used for the rest of the meeting and afterwards.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment adopted:} Case 6 on 26H-ECL-GA-CASE5A, Case 7 on 26H-ECL-GA-CASE6A with ballot slips 26H-ECL-GA-BALLOT2A. From signature of the Meeting Protocol, the New Bylaws apply, and new members sign 26H-ECL-GA-MEMB1; responsibilities and Signature Right are transferred with 26H-ECL-GA-RESP1B.
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment not adopted:} Case 6 on 26H-ECL-GA-CASE5B, Case 7 on 26H-ECL-GA-CASE6B with ballot slips 26H-ECL-GA-BALLOT2B. The Old Bylaws remain in force, and the Board records joint signing decisions with 26H-ECL-GA-RESP1A.
\end{itemize}
