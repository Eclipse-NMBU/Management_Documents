\vsection{4. After the Bylaw Amendment vote}
Case 7 decides which documents are used for the rest of the meeting and afterwards.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment adopted:} Case 8 on ECL-H2026-CASE-08A, Case 9 on ECL-H2026-CASE-06A with ballot slips ECL-H2026-BALLOT-02A. From signature of the Meeting Protocol, the New Bylaws apply, and new members sign ECL-H2026-MEMB-01; responsibilities and Signature Right are transferred with ECL-H2026-RESP-01B.
  \item \textbf{Bylaw Amendment not adopted:} Case 8 on ECL-H2026-CASE-08B, Case 9 on ECL-H2026-CASE-06B with ballot slips ECL-H2026-BALLOT-02B. The Old Bylaws remain in force, and the Board records joint signing decisions with ECL-H2026-RESP-01A.
\end{itemize}
